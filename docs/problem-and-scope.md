# Problem and Scope

## Problem
Manual land-document screening can require cross-page comparison and review of conflicting fields, unusual image patterns, or metadata. BhuSatya helps surface review indicators with evidence.

## User need
“When I upload a land document, show me what deserves closer review, why it was flagged, and where the evidence appears.”

## MVP scope
PDF/JPG/PNG upload, file validation, SHA-256, OCR adapter, extracted-field comparison, synthetic registry lookup, seven-category risk aggregation, explainable report and disclaimers.

## Non-goals
No real government database connection, government portal scraping, legal authenticity decision, proof of title/ownership, or unsupported accuracy claims.

## Disclaimers
English: “AI-generated screening result. This assessment is not legal certification, proof of ownership, or a substitute for verification with the competent authority.”
Hindi: “एआई-जनरेटेड स्क्रीनिंग परिणाम। यह मूल्यांकन कानूनी प्रमाणन, स्वामित्व का प्रमाण या सक्षम प्राधिकारी के सत्यापन का विकल्प नहीं है।”
